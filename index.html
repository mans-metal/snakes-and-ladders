HOW TO PLAY

THE CAST

The colours are just for looks: anyone can climb any ladder and slide down any snake.

THE RULES

  1. Play alone or in a team. Pick a chit: that's your personality, and your pawn.
  2. Roll a die, or press Roll, and move. Drag your pawn on screen to match.
  3. Foot of a ladder: read the card out loud and climb.
  4. Head of a snake: read the card, then try the escape challenge. Pass and you stay put. Fail and you slide.
  5. has the final say on every challenge.
  6. First to square wins. Keep going if there are cards left to read.

Click any snake or ladder to read its card.
